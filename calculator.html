About the calculator

The calculator looks for the mix of individual source samples that comes closest to your coordinates, the same way Vahaduo does it with 500 slots, and then adds the samples up by group. It averages 16 such runs, so the same coordinates always give the same numbers. With this many individual samples most Balkan populations fit almost exactly, so very small fit distances are normal.

For the pre-Slavic profile, the Slavic part of your best mix is subtracted from your coordinates and the rest is modelled again with the other sources. The larger your Slavic share, the less stable this gets.

Data and credits

The calculator models and the modern and ancient averages come from Makedonas G25 (codeberg.org/MAKEDONAS/MAKEDONAS-G25). The coordinates are in the Global25 system by Davidski (Eurogenes), and the fitting method follows Vahaduo Admixture JS.
