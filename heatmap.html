2How it works

  1. Compare. Your coordinates are compared with about 1,700 samples; a lower distance means more similar.
  2. Place. Each sample sits at its homeland. Diaspora and outlier groups are left out. “Show samples” under the map marks them.
  3. Fill in. The space between the populations is filled in smoothly (kriging).
  4. Colour. Land is coloured from red (closest) to dark blue (the maximum distance or more). Lines mark steps of 0.01.

4Questions

What are G25 coordinates?

Global25 (G25), from the Eurogenes project by Davidski, summarises a person’s autosomal DNA as 25 numbers. The closer two sets of numbers are, the more similar the people behind them.

How do I get my coordinates?

Send the raw data of a DNA test (for example AncestryDNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage or FamilyTreeDNA) to a G25 service such as g25requests.app, and use the scaled coordinates it returns. This site is not affiliated with any of them and only needs the line with the 25 numbers.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your coordinates are processed only on your device. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics; only your light or dark choice is stored, in your own browser.

What do the colours and numbers mean?

Red marks the places whose people are genetically closest to you, dark blue the maximum distance or more. As a rough guide, below 0.02 is a very close match, around 0.05 is typical between different parts of Europe, and 0.10 or more is far apart.
