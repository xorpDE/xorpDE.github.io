How it works

  1. Compare

    Your coordinates are compared with every population of the chosen dataset. A lower distance means more similar.

  2. Place

    Each population sits at its homeland; diaspora and outlier groups are left out. “Show populations” marks them on your map.

  3. Fill in

    The space between the populations is filled in smoothly (kriging).

  4. Colour

    Land is coloured along the scale under the map, from the closest places to the maximum distance or more. Lines mark steps of 0.01, wider on wide scales.

Questions

How do I get my coordinates?

Send the raw data file of a DNA test (for example AncestryDNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage or FamilyTreeDNA) to a G25 service such as g25requests.app, and paste the scaled coordinates it returns: one line with a name and 25 numbers. This site is not affiliated with any of these services.

What are G25 coordinates?

Global25 (G25), from the Eurogenes project by Davidski, summarises a person’s DNA as 25 numbers. The closer two sets of numbers are, the more similar the people behind them.

What do the colours and numbers mean?

Each place is coloured by how close its native population is to your coordinates; the scale under the map shows which colour means close. The numbers are genetic distances. As a rough guide, below 0.02 is a very close match, around 0.05 is typical between different parts of Europe, and 0.10 or more is far apart.

Why is my map almost all one colour?

The maximum distance does not suit this map. If almost everything has the farthest colour, choose a larger maximum distance under More options (world maps use 0.30 automatically). If almost everything looks close, choose a smaller one.

Which dataset should I choose?

Advanced Moriopoulos and Makedonas G25 are two separate collections of averages of modern populations. Start with the default; the other gives a second view, and the results can differ slightly.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your coordinates stay on your device: the map is computed in your browser. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics; only your light or dark choice is saved, in your browser. The map engine itself (Pyodide) is downloaded from the jsDelivr network and never receives your coordinates.

Data and credits

  • Population averages: Advanced Moriopoulos (Moriopoulos Collection) and Makedonas G25, in the Global25 system by Davidski (Eurogenes).
  • Land outlines: Natural Earth, 1:10m and 1:50m land, public domain. naturalearthdata.com
  • In your browser: Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly). pyodide.org
  • Computing and drawing: NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib; map lettering in the Inter typeface (SIL Open Font License).
  • Method: ordinary kriging (G. Matheron, 1963), computed with a PyKrige-compatible implementation.

For curiosity and learning: the map shows genetic similarity to modern populations, not identity, nationality or health.
