Balkan PCA plot

Shows where you fall among modern Balkan populations and ancient ones from the Balkans and elsewhere.

How do I get them?

One sample per line: a name and 25 comma-separated numbers. Every line becomes a point.

Scroll or pinch to zoom and drag to move. Hover over or tap a dot to see its name, or zoom in to see all names. After clicking the plot you can also use the arrow keys, + and -.

How to read the plot

Populations that sit close together are genetically similar. The plot only shows the main directions of variation, so two populations can look close here and still differ in the full 25 coordinates. The distance table in the calculator uses all of them.

The first axis runs from the Mediterranean on the left to the Slavic north on the right, the second from the East Mediterranean at the bottom to the Paleo-Balkan populations at the top. PC3 and PC4 show smaller differences. You can switch to them with the X and Y buttons.
